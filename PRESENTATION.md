# Afterglow Demo Presentation
## Study Guide for Lisbon Music Hackspace Hackathon

---

## SLIDE 1: Title Slide

**Content:** Afterglow: Organize Your Library the Way You Actually DJ

**Speaker Notes (30 sec):**
Open with energy. Introduce yourself, the team (all-women), and the one problem: DJs know their tracks but aren't sure how to organize them across multiple moments. Set the hook: "This is about changing that moment."

---

## SLIDE 2: Three Scenarios, Same Realization

**Content:**

**SCENARIO 1: Club Reading the Room**
- 10pm → arrivals (slow, groovy)
- 11pm → locked in (take risks)
- Midnight → reset (new crowd)
- Your mental model shifts three times in four hours

**SCENARIO 2: Festival Opener (Uncertain Slot)**
- 48 hours to be ready for: sunset (chill) OR peak (high energy) OR afterparty (experimental)
- Same library, completely different vibes

**SCENARIO 3: The Pattern**
Any DJ working across multiple moments asks the same question: "For THIS moment, with THIS crowd, at THIS energy—which track am I confident about?"

**The Gap:**
Tools organize by collection (playlists). You think by context (moments).

**Speaker Notes (1.5 min):**
Lead with club (most immediate). Each scenario gets 20 sec. Land the insight at the end: the tool-brain mismatch. Don't explain—let them feel the tension. Pause before "THE GAP."

---

## SLIDE 3: The Shift—From Collection to Confidence

**Content:**

**Current Thinking:** "Is this a Wedding track or an Afro House track?" (Binary choice)

**Your Brain:** Works in layers, confidence levels, across moments.

**The Insight:**
- Not "what IS this track?"
- But "when am I CONFIDENT about this track?"

**Example:**
One track → Club warm-up (9/10) + Festival peak (7/10) + Wedding reception (6/10)  
= Describing reality, not changing the track.

**Speaker Notes (45 sec):**
This is THE moment they get it. Pause. Let it land. Emphasize: you're not lying about the track, you're describing where it shines. This shift is why confidence builds.

---

## SLIDE 4: Step 1—Define Your Contexts

**Content:**

- **Club DJ:** Warm-up, Lock-in, Reset, Peak
- **Festival Opener:** Sunrise, Peak, Sunset, Afterparty
- **Track Selector:** Discovery, Build, Momentum, Release
- **Radio DJ:** Commute, Work day, Evening, Late night

**YOUR library. YOUR categories. YOUR language. (~1 minute setup)**

**Speaker Notes (1.5 min):**
Emphasize ownership—this is their first intentional choice about their sound. Not genre. Not BPM. Moments. Makes them feel heard.

---

## SLIDE 5: Step 2—Tag with Intention

### 🎬 LIVE DEMO: Tagging UX Showcase

**Content:**

**THREE DIMENSIONS (each rated 1-10, honest):**

1. **YOUR SETTING** (Warm-up, Lock-in, etc.) — How confident?
2. **JOURNEY ROLE** (Opener, Bridge, Reset, Home Stretch) — How confident?
3. **CROWD STATE** (Arrivals, Lock-in, Wanderers) — How confident?

**Example: Club Lock-in track**
- Setting: 9/10 confident
- Journey Role: Peak (8/10)
- Crowd State: Lock-in (7/10)

**Same Track, Festival Context**
- Setting: Festival Sunset (6/10)
- Journey Role: Bridge (5/10)
- Crowd State: Arrivals (4/10)

→ Precision. Honesty. Craft begins here.

**Speaker Notes (2 min):**
Walk through the thinking—this is where real work happens. Emphasize: honesty, not perfection. Show that one track naturally fits multiple contexts with different confidence levels. This is the aha moment about nuance.

---

## SLIDE 6: Step 3—See Your Library

### 🎬 LIVE DEMO: Constellation UI Visualization

**Content:**

**Constellation Visualization:**
- **X-axis:** JOURNEY ROLE (Opener ← → Home Stretch)
- **Y-axis:** CROWD STATE (Arrivals ← → Wanderers)
- Every tagged track = a point in space (filtered by setting)

**WHAT YOU SEE:**
- **Clusters:** "I'm strong in Opener + Arrivals"
- **Outliers:** "That Home Stretch + Wanderers track is interesting"
- **Gaps:** "I need Bridge + Lock-in tracks"

→ You see your sound. Your taste. Your library.

**Speaker Notes (1.5 min):**
Paint the visual picture. Make them imagine it. For emerging DJs, this moment is transformative: they realize their taste isn't random—it's coherent. It's theirs.

## SLIDE 7: The Magic Moment—Discovery

**Content:**

You're exploring your constellation.  
Click a Club Warm-up track.  
It's spatially near your Festival Peak tracks.

You look at the ratings: "Oh—7/10 for Festival Peak, 6/10 for Lock-in crowd."

**Realization:** "I could use this in my Peak set. Not guessing. Already rated. I know why it works. I'm confident about it."

→ Preparation meeting serendipity.  
→ The moment you trust your taste.

**Speaker Notes (1 min):**
Make them feel the moment. This is the emotional peak of the pitch. Pause before the realization. Let confidence become tangible.

## SLIDE 8: Why This Matters

**Content:**

✓ **Understand your own taste** (not someone else's categories)  
✓ **Ready for the unexpected** (you've already done the thinking)  
✓ **Intentionality** (judges hear confidence, not guessing)  
✓ **Build faster** (you know your gaps, know what to dig for)  
✓ **Collaborate with precision** ("This track kills in Lock-in + Peak space" vs. "it's tech house")

→ This separates emerging DJs from lasting DJs.

**Speaker Notes (1 min):**
Don't read the list. Hit 2-3 points that resonate with your judges' world. Speed matters—these are benefits, not arguments. Land the final line with conviction.

**Total Time:** 5-7 minutes + Q&A

**Key Moments:**
- **Slide 2:** Feel the problem (tension)
- **Slide 3:** Understand the insight (aha)
- **Slide 5:** See the tagging tool (live demo)
- **Slide 6:** See the constellation (live demo, emotional peak)

- **Slide 8:** Understand the why (belief)
- **Slide 11:** Land the promise (conviction)

**Tone Throughout:** Confident, authentic, craft-focused. No AI speak. Natural language.
