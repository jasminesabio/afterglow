**Notes:** Pause here. This is the aha moment. Emphasize: you're describing reality, not lying about the track.

# Branding Guide

## Color Palette

| Purpose | Color | Hex | Use |
|---------|-------|-----|-----|
| Primary | Deep Teal | #0D9488 | Headings, accents, key emphasis |
| Dark | Charcoal | #0F172A | Dark backgrounds, body text |
| Light | Off-white | #F8F7F5 | Content background |
| Accent | Gold | #D4AF37 | Callouts, highlights, dividers |
| Text | Dark Gray | #404040 | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Muted | Gray | #999999 | Secondary text, captions |

## Typography

**Headings:** Inter Bold or Calibri Bold (24-44pt)  
**Body:** Inter Regular or Calibri (14-18pt)  
**Speaker Notes:** Inter Italic, Gray (12pt)

**Tone:** Clean, modern, accessible. No AI speak. Natural language.

## Visual Style

- **Minimal decoration** — White space over ornament
- **One accent per slide** — Color bar, highlight, or shape (not multiple)
- **Icons:** Simple, single-color, left of text
- **Images:** Real screenshots of tagging UI + constellation viz (no stock photos)
- **Consistency:** Same fonts, same color use, same spacing throughout

## Slide Template Suggestion

```
[Dark teal accent bar on left edge (0.08" wide)]
[White background for content]
[Title: 40pt Inter Bold, teal, top]
[Content: 16pt Inter Regular, dark gray, left-aligned]
[Key callout: Gold accent or highlight]
```

## Design Notes

✓ Every slide needs a visual (demo screenshot, diagram, or shape)  
✓ No dense text blocks — break into scannable chunks  
✓ Title + 3-4 key points max per slide  
✓ Dark teal background for title slide only (dark/light sandwich)  
✓ Gold dividers or callout boxes for key moments  
✓ Clean, confident. Judges should feel you know your craft.
